Zoo and Game Jam Awards

📅 May 18, 2026👤 By Sebastiaan Vermeulen

Day 6 in Stuttgart was a study in contrasts, moving from the quiet observation of nature in the morning to the high-energy anticipation of the game jam awards in the evening.

We started the day at Wilhelma, Stuttgart's famous zoological-botanical garden. It is a massive, historic site, and even with a full morning, it felt like we were only scratching the surface. The weather held up well enough to see a significant variety of wildlife. We spent a good deal of time watching the pelicans preening by the water and checking out the heavy hitters of the animal kingdom. The giraffes and elephants are always impressive in person, but there was something particularly captivating about seeing the snow leopard and the black panther. Both cats were remarkably calm, though the panther looked perfectly at home lounging on a high branch. We also managed to catch a glimpse of a gorilla and a spectacled bear, making for a very successful circuit of the park.

As the sun began to set, the mood shifted from leisure to business—or at least the social side of it. We headed over to reBOOTS bar for the official post-jam wrap-up party. After days of intensive development and little sleep, the atmosphere was thick with a mix of exhaustion and excitement. This was the moment everyone had been waiting for: the announcement of the winners.

I’ll be honest: I didn't take home a prize. It is always a bit of a sting when you’ve put your soul into a project and it doesn't get the nod from the judges, but that’s the nature of the beast. However, it was a massive night for the Howest crew. My peers absolutely dominated the podium, sweeping the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place spots with their respective groups. Seeing the talent from our school recognized like that was incredible, and it definitely softened the blow of my own loss.

The rest of the night was a blur of celebratory drinks, post-mortem discussions about what worked and what didn't, and the kind of bonding that only happens after a week-long sprint. We stayed until the very end, soaking in the last bit of the Stuttgart jam scene before finally admitting defeat to our fatigue and heading back to our accommodation. It was a long day, but a memorable way to wrap up this leg of the trip.
